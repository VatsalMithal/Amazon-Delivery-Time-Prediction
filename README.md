# Amazon Delivery Time Prediction

## Project Description

The **Amazon Delivery Time Prediction** project focuses on predicting the estimated delivery time of orders using data analysis, **time series analysis**, and **machine learning techniques**. The goal of this project is to analyze delivery-related datasets and develop predictive insights that help understand how different factors affect delivery time.
Delivery time prediction plays a crucial role in logistics and supply chain management. Accurate predictions help companies improve operational efficiency, optimize delivery routes, and enhance customer satisfaction. **Time series analysis helps identify patterns over time**, while machine learning models analyze historical delivery data and influencing factors to estimate delivery times more accurately.
This project demonstrates how **data science, time-based analysis, and predictive modeling** can transform logistics data into meaningful insights that support better business decision-making.

# Problem Statement

In the e-commerce industry, delivery time is one of the most important factors influencing customer satisfaction. Delays in delivery can lead to poor customer experience and inefficiencies in logistics operations.
This project aims to:
* Analyze delivery datasets to identify important factors affecting delivery time
* Apply **time series analysis to understand delivery patterns over time**
* Use **machine learning techniques to predict delivery time**
* Generate insights that can help optimize logistics operations
* Improve understanding of delivery performance trends
Machine learning and time-based analytics are increasingly used in logistics to analyze delivery patterns, forecast estimated arrival times (ETA), and optimize supply chain operations.

# Key Insights

* Delivery time tends to increase with **longer delivery distances and higher order volumes**, indicating the importance of route planning and logistics optimization.

* Certain locations show **consistent delivery delays**, which may indicate traffic congestion, infrastructure limitations, or inefficient route allocation.

* **Time-based analysis reveals peak delivery hours**, during which delivery times tend to increase due to higher order demand and operational pressure.

* Some features such as **distance, order time, and delivery partner availability** have a strong influence on delivery time prediction.

* Applying **time series analysis helps identify patterns and trends over time**, which can support better demand forecasting and delivery scheduling.

These insights demonstrate how **data analysis, time series patterns, and predictive modeling can support logistics optimization and improve delivery efficiency in e-commerce operations.**
